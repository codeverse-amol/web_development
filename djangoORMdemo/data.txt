🔷 Step 1: Identify relationships (VERY IMPORTANT)

Before writing code, always think:

🧠 Real-world relationships =>
Entity A        Entity B	        Relation
User        	Profile	            OneToOne
User	        Orders	            OneToMany
Order	        Product	            ManyToMany
Product     	Category	        ForeignKey
Product	        Tags	            ManyToMany
User	        Cart	            OneToOne
Cart	        Products	        ManyToMany
